Privacy Policy for SlidePix

Developer: GM Nova Studio
Last updated: November 19, 2026

GM Nova Studio ("we", "us") provides SlidePix, an offline sliding-puzzle Android game. This policy explains how the current free version handles information on your device.

1. Information used by the app

Photos you choose: If you select "My Photo", Android's system file/photo picker lets you choose an image. SlidePix reads the selected image to create your puzzle and stores a processed copy in the app's private storage so you can play or resume it. SlidePix does not automatically scan your photo library.

Game information: SlidePix stores puzzle progress, scores and best times, game preferences (language, sound, vibration) and selected puzzle settings locally on your device.

Accounts: SlidePix does not require user registration or a login.

2. Collection, sharing and online services

The current version of SlidePix does not send your selected photographs, game progress or preferences to servers operated by GM Nova Studio. It does not include advertising, analytics, user tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and it is designed to work without an internet connection.

Android and Google Play are separate services operated by their respective providers. They may process app-installation, security, diagnostic or device information under their own privacy terms outside the control of SlidePix.

3. Storage and backups

Game progress, records and preferences are stored in SlidePix's private app storage. Depending on your Android settings and device, some preferences and progress may be included in Android's backup or device-transfer features. In the version containing this policy, SlidePix is configured to exclude app-created copies of your personal photos from Android's app backup and device-transfer rules. Backup behavior is ultimately managed by the operating system and backup provider.

4. Retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device while the app is installed unless Android clears it or the app replaces it. You can remove locally stored SlidePix data through Android Settings > Apps > SlidePix > Storage > Clear data, or by uninstalling SlidePix. This deletes local game progress and stored photo copies; it may not remove copies already held by your device's backup service. Consult Android or your backup provider to manage existing backups.

Because SlidePix does not operate user accounts or collect this information on GM Nova Studio servers, we cannot retrieve or delete your locally stored game data remotely.

5. Children and family use

SlidePix is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you choose a personal photograph, the image is used locally to create the puzzle as described above.

6. Security

SlidePix uses Android's private application storage for saved photographs and game data. Access to this storage is controlled by Android. No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. We will publish the revised text at this page and update the date above. If future versions introduce ads, analytics or online functionality, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety information before or when those changes apply.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about SlidePix, contact GM Nova Studio at gmnovastudio@gmail.com.
